Total Battle Auto-Crypter

Let your crypts take care of themselves.

Tired of constantly checking your crypts, selecting the right type, sending commanders and waiting for them to return? Auto-Crypter automates the crypting process for you.

Set your preferences, start the tool, and let it handle your crypts while you focus on playing the game.

What does Auto-Crypter do?

  • Automatic crypt selection — automatically handles the crypts available to you.
  • Crypt type selection — choose which types of crypts you want to run.
  • Tartaros support — Tartaros crypts are handled automatically.
  • Up to 3 commanders — automatically manage up to three commanders.
  • Automatic commander management — waits for commanders to return before sending them again.
  • Second-crypt support — send two commanders to a second crypt when necessary.
  • Crypt filters — exclude crypts you don't want to run.
  • Smart scrolling — if wheel scrolling is blocked by touchpad or mouse drivers, Auto-Crypter can drag the crypt list instead.
  • Multiple resolutions supported — with more resolutions added based on user requests.
  • Unlimited crypting — no limits on marches, crypts or hours of use.
