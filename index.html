Total Battle Auto-Crypter

Hit your crypting target without babysitting a crypt run.

You know the drill: scroll the watchtower, find a crypt, pick the type, send a commander, wait, repeat — for as long as it takes to hit whatever your clan expects from you. It's not hard, it's just tedious, and it ties up your attention the whole time.

Auto-Crypter does that loop for you. Set your preferences once, hit start, and it works through your crypts on its own — so you consistently hit your numbers without a dedicated crypting session eating your evening.

Hit clan targets, consistently

Runs through your crypts the same reliable way every time, so you're not scrambling to catch up before a deadline.

Get your time back

What used to be a dedicated sitting is now something that runs on your computer while you do something else.

One less thing to juggle

No more tabbing back and forth to click through crypts between everything else you're doing.

Skip the boring part

The repetitive clicking is the least fun part of crypting anyway — let the tool grind it out instead of you.

How it works

  • You tell it what to run. Pick your commanders (up to three), choose which crypt types to include, and filter out the ones you don't want. It also works on Tartaros crypts.
  • It finds the crypts for you. Auto-Crypter reads your watchtower list and works through it on its own and navigates to the crypt. It will find it even if a crypt shows up off-center on screen.
  • It doesn't stop until you want it to. No caps on marches, crypts, or hours — run it for a quick top-up or let it carry a full session.

Works at 1024×768, 1366×768, 1680×1050, 1760×990, and 1920×1080. Need another resolution? Email support@ccc-hq.com. Full setup details are in the installation guide.

Enter your Leadership, Dominance and troop stats once, pick the troops you want to send, and the Battle Calculator works out how many of each to bring and in which order they attack — so you spend your capacity instead of guessing at it.
