TB Auto-Crypt

An automated crypt-running assistant for Total Battle. It finds and opens available crypts on the map, sends your captains to explore them, and manages the whole cycle — march, explore, return — hands-free.

  • Signs in with your Google account — no separate password to manage
  • Runs multiple captains per round, one after another
  • Optional speed-ups and crypt filtering
  • Subscription-based access; price will be announced soon
  • For now limited for Windows systems, with a fixed resolution. More platforms and resolutions will become available soon.
