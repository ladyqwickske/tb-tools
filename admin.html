cCc members

Approved members get the Battle Calculator free and 50% off Auto-Crypt. Revoking removes calculator access immediately and drops the discount from their next renewal.

STRIPE_COUPON_CCC_AUTOCRYPT isn't set on the Worker, so approved members currently pay full price for Auto-Crypt. The free calculator works regardless.

Members per clan

Approved cCc members and active grandfathered users, grouped by the clan set for them. Set someone's clan with the clan picker on their card.

Clan list check

Every day the members on this page are looked up on the clans' own member lists (Members, Former Members and the rename history on each clan's site), by linked Google account first, then by name or old name. Moving to another cCc clan or a rename is followed; only someone on no clan's Members list is listed here and emailed to you, once.

Grandfathered users

Full access without a subscription: the grandfathered_users table and the old GitHub allowlist. They can receive lists; allow them here to send lists too. Use Name… to set the name others see them under (send dialog, "sent by").

Can send lists to members

These people (and every admin) get a Send to member… button in the Battle Calculator: it puts a list with its registration straight into another member's calculator, online and in the desktop app.

Admins

Admins can use this page (cCc approvals, senders, names) and open players' calculators on Players' calculators. Only grandfathered users can become admins, and only the owner can make or remove them.
