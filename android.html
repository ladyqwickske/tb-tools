Android App

Auto-Crypter on your phone

TB Auto-Crypt runs on your Android phone next to the Total Battle app, no PC needed. It's part of your Auto-Crypter subscription. Here's everything from download to your first run, in about 10 minutes.

What you'll need 1. Download 2. Install 3. Sign in 4. Permissions 5. Settings 6. Run it Need help?

What you'll need

  • An Android phone with Android 8 or newer (64-bit, as nearly every phone from recent years is).
  • The Total Battle app installed and signed in on that phone, set to English. Auto-Crypter recognises the English game screens only. The game runs in portrait on phones, and so does the app.
  • An Auto-Crypter or All-In subscription (or trial), and the Google account you subscribed with. The Android app is included: use the PC app, the Android app, or both, with the same account for the same price.
  • A free device slot: your phone counts as one of your 2 devices. Using it on your PC as well? That's your 2. Remove an old device on My Account.
  • For long runs, the phone on its charger: the screen stays on while it crypts.
Not from the Play Store: Google doesn't allow game automation apps in the Play Store, so you install TB Auto-Crypt from this page. Android will warn you about that and about the permissions it needs. The steps below explain each one.
1

Download the app

Open this page on your phone and tap the button. It downloads one file, TB-AutoCrypt-<version>.apk.

Download v1.0.0 for Android

Updates: the app tells you itself when a new version is out, with a Download button. Installing it keeps your settings and sign-in.

2

Install it

  1. Open the downloaded file, from your browser's downloads or its notification.
  2. Android asks to allow installing apps from your browser: tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, and go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. Open TB Auto-Crypt.
3

Agree to the terms and sign in

  1. Read the warning: Total Battle's own rules don't allow automation tools, so using one is at your own risk (Terms of Service, section 4). Tick I agree and tap Continue.
  2. Tap Sign in with Google and choose the Google account of your subscription, the same one you use on your PC and on this website.
  3. It shows Signed in as … — subscription OK. If it says you've reached the 2-device limit, remove an old device on My Account and tap Try again.
4

Allow what it needs

The app lists each step with a button that opens the right Android screen, and shows ✓ done once it's set.

  1. Notifications → Allow. Shows that it's watching the screen, with Stop buttons.
  2. Tapping in the game. On Android 13 and newer, first tap App info → ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings; without it the next switch stays grey. Then Accessibility → Installed apps → TB Auto-Crypt → on. This is how it taps and swipes in the game.
  3. Battery → Allow. So Android doesn't stop a run while the phone sits idle.
  4. Screen capture → Start → Start now. This is how it sees the game. If Android offers a choice, take Entire screen. Android asks again each time the app starts.
What it does with these: while a run is on, it looks at the Total Battle screen and taps in it, keeps the screen on, and notices which app is in front so it can reopen the game if it closes. It sends nothing from your phone anywhere except the sign-in and subscription check.
5

Choose your settings

Tap Run settings: the same options as on the PC.

  • Captains per exploration (1 to 3), whether to explore opened rare crypts, speed-ups, and 2 captains to the second crypt.
  • A limit: none, a duration, or a number of marches.
  • The crypt types to ignore, with their pictures.

Save at the top, or just go back: that saves too.

6

Run it

  • Start in the game on the map or in your city. Start run opens Total Battle and begins after 5 seconds.
  • Leave the phone alone while it crypts. If the game closes or crashes, the app opens it again and carries on.
  • Stop it with Stop run in the notification, or in the app.
  • Something looks off? Tap Share log in the app and email it to support@ccc-hq.com.

Need a hand?

Stuck on a step? Email support@ccc-hq.com, with the log from Share log if the app already runs.
